from the Arabic — radiant, shining

Ten of the strangest, wildest, most beautiful places on earth to sit in the heat, plunge into the cold, and be covered head to toe in mud. Assembled for an audience of one.

There’s a version of this that begins in Cambridge — and ends, the way the best stories do, in Morocco.

You said someone ought to find the best saunas in the world. I took that more seriously than you probably intended. Ten places: charred timber on Nordic lakes, volcanic mud the Māori have trusted for seven centuries, a bathhouse so beautiful it feels like trespassing, and one hot spring you can sit in for free at three in the morning. Every one chosen for a reason. Most of those reasons are green.

This isn’t a plan and it isn’t a proposal. It’s a shortlist — and shortlists are far better argued about in person, over something good to eat. Pick the one you’d fight for. We can take the other nine apart properly.

So — I was wondering whether you fancied dinner.

To talk all of this through properly, and mostly to get to know you better. I won’t be the least bit offended if it’s a no — I haven’t been in this situation for some time. But I thought I’d ask, because if I didn’t, well. Who knows.

I love food, and I cook. So tell me the best thing you’ve ever eaten, and the one place you’ve been meaning to try. You choose where — I’ll book it.
