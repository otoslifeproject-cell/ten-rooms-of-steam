A shortlist · nine rooms of steam

Leemya

from the Arabic — radiant, shining

Nine of the strangest, wildest, most beautiful places on earth to sit in the heat, plunge into the cold, and be covered head to toe in mud — sorted by how much of your time they would actually cost. Assembled for an audience of one.

Begin

There’s a version of this that begins an hour from Cambridge — and ends, the way the best stories do, in Morocco.

You said someone ought to find the best saunas in the world. I took that more seriously than you intended. But you are a busy woman, so they are sorted by what they would cost you rather than what they cost: an hour, a weekend, or a week neither of us has. The first three are real. The last three are for dreaming. Every one was chosen for a reason, and most of those reasons are green.

Entirely hypothetical, obviously.

Nothing is booked — that’s rather the point

Nine doors. Steam behind every one.
Which would you argue for?

This isn’t a plan and it isn’t a proposal. It’s a shortlist — and shortlists are far better argued about in person, over something good to eat. Pick the one you’d fight for. We can take the other eight apart properly.

So — I was wondering whether you fancied dinner.

To talk all of this through properly, and mostly to get to know you better. I won’t be the least bit offended if it’s a no — I haven’t been in this situation for some time. But I thought I’d ask, because if I didn’t, well. Who knows.

I love food, and I cook. So tell me the best thing you’ve ever eaten, and the one place you’ve been meaning to try. You choose where — I’ll book it.

You’ve got my number.

For Leemya — radiant, shining.
Every shade of green in these pages was chosen on purpose.

— Dean
